The three problems

parser: day and month swap. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. 7 April arrives as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 describes, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it asks, and the form reads the date day first, the tenant’s way. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here. A correct parser does not fix it: the hint must match the order the form reads.

shift: one day early. These reports say a date moved by one day, not that day and month swapped: a Tuesday shown as Monday, a meeting held on two days for clients in Lisbon and Toronto, an invite that arrived before its booking. That is a time-zone conversion. The desk noted them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and count them under the parser cause by tenant, so the parser row includes them. They still need their own fix.

How to read a date report

  • Count unique report ids. The ticket export pages overlap, so one report can appear twice.
  • The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause follows the tenant’s locale, its booking form and the channel (causes.ts).
  • Read the body before you merge. A one-day move and a swapped date share a subject line, not a fix.
